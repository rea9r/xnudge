# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-10_

xnudge does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

## What we store

xnudge uses Chrome's `storage.sync` API to remember your preferences:

- Whether the theme override is enabled
- The active preset
- Your custom palette colors, if you've edited them

These values are stored by Chrome and synced across the devices where you are signed in with the same Google account. They never leave Chrome.

## What we do not do

- No data collection, telemetry, or analytics.
- No communication with external servers.
- No reading or modification of x.com page content beyond injecting our own theme stylesheet.

## Permissions

- `storage`: required to remember your toggle state.
- Host permissions on `x.com` and `twitter.com`: required to inject the theme stylesheet on those domains.
